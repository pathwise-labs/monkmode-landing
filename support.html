Frequently Asked Questions
How do I block apps?
Open Monk Mode → tap the App Shield card on the home screen → grant Screen Time permission when prompted → choose the apps you want blocked. The shield activates automatically when you start a Monk session or hit your daily screen time limit.
How do I unlock an app early?
Tap a blocked app to see the Monk Mode shield, then tap "Emergency Override." You get up to 3 overrides per day — each one is logged to your Crew so they can hold you accountable. Repeat use breaks your Clean Days streak.
How do I cancel my Pro subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside Monk Mode. On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Monk Mode → Cancel Subscription. Your Pro features remain active until the end of the billing period.
How do I delete my account?
Open Monk Mode → Settings tab → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your profile, posts, habits, and streaks. The action cannot be undone. If you need help with this, email us at hello@pathwiseacademy.co.
How do I report a user or post?
Tap the three-dot menu on any post in the Crew feed → choose "Report" → select a reason (spam, harassment, hate speech, offensive content). Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block any user from the same menu — blocked users will no longer see your activity.
I forgot my password or can't sign in
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link. If you signed up with Apple, use the "Sign in with Apple" button — there's no separate password to remember. Still stuck? Email hello@pathwiseacademy.co with the email address on your account.
My habit didn't log / streak broke
Habits log to your local device first, then sync to the cloud when you have a connection. If a habit didn't appear in your Crew feed, check that you're online and pull-to-refresh the feed. If your streak shows incorrectly after a sync, email us with your username and the date in question and we'll fix it manually.
The App Shield isn't blocking apps
Make sure you've granted Screen Time / Family Controls permission: iOS Settings → Screen Time → Lock Screen Time Settings should be off, then re-open Monk Mode and re-grant permission when asked. The shield only works on iOS 16 and later.
Bug reports & feature requests
Send anything you've noticed to hello@pathwiseacademy.co. Screenshots, your iOS version, and a short description of what happened are super helpful. We're a small team and read every message.
