How it works

Earn your apps back,
every single day.

Monk Mode flips the script — instead of relying on willpower alone, your apps stay locked until you prove you deserve them.

1

Add your habits

Choose from 30+ categories — gym, reading, meditation, journalling, cold shower, and more. Set your schedule and daily targets.

2

Block distracting apps

Select which apps to lock — social media, games, streaming. Monk Mode uses Apple's Screen Time to enforce the block automatically.

3

Log habits to unlock

Complete your habits and log them in the app. Hit your daily goal and your blocked apps unlock automatically as your reward.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Monk Mode free? +

Yes — Monk Mode is free to download and use. Core features including habit tracking, app blocking, streaks, and the crew feed are all free.

How does the app blocking actually work? +

Monk Mode uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls) to block the apps you choose. This is the same technology built into iOS — it can't be bypassed by simply deleting Monk Mode. The blocking runs directly on your device and we never have access to which apps you block.

What happens if I don't log my habits? +

Your blocked apps stay locked until you log your habits. You'll get an evening reminder at 8pm if you haven't logged yet. Miss a full day and your streak resets to zero — that's the point.

Can I keep my habits private? +

Yes. When you log a habit you choose whether to share it to your crew feed or keep it private. Nothing is shared automatically. Your app blocking data never leaves your device.

What is the Focus Timer? +

The Focus Timer (Monk Timer) lets you set a deep work session of any length. While it's running, your apps stay blocked and a live countdown appears on your Dynamic Island and lock screen so you stay aware without having to open the app.

Is it available on Android? +

Monk Mode is currently iOS only. The app blocking feature relies on Apple's Screen Time API which isn't available on Android. An Android version may come in the future.

How do I delete my account? +

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing privacy@monkmode.app. We process deletion requests within 30 days.
